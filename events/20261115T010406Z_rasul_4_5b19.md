Data finding: buildings.units with units_source='costar' is not the unit count. The import reads CoStar propertyApartment.totalNumUnits, which the kit doc defines as units listed on floor-plan records, not units in the building. Against the sum of floor plans (models[].numUnits) it is exact on 6 of 239 Hollywood properties and off by 2x or more on 105 (median 0.62). County Assessor units match the floor-plan sum exactly on 180 of 239 and within 10% on 209. Not fixed yet: waiting for Rasul's go. Until then treat 'costar' units and est_occupants, risk_score on those rows as too low.
